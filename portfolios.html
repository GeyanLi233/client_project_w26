Showcase Your Process and Professional Identity

Successful Portfolios Help You Succeed

A portfolio is a collection of your past work, illustrating your process of delivering high-quality projects and showcasing your capabilities, professional skills, and experience.

Portfolio Guidance

Portfolio Requirements by Pathway

  • User Experience (UX): Portfolios are generally required.
  • Data: Portfolios are occasionally required.
  • Library/Archives: Portfolios are typically not mandatory.
  • Health Informatics: Portfolios are typically not mandatory.

Portfolio Components Explained

  • Front Page: Keep it clean and attractive. Feature three key projects to intrigue potential employers immediately.
  • About Me: Showcase your personality and background so employers get to know the person behind the work.
  • Projects: Demonstrate your work in depth—include your role, client, duration, and the specific challenge you solved.
  • Navigation: Employers have limited time; make the user experience seamless with accessible links and clear labels.

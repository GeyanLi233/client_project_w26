Showcase Your Process and Professional Identity

Successful Portfolios Help You Succeed

A portfolio is a collection of your past work, illustrating your process of delivering high-quality projects and showcasing your capabilities, professional skills, and experience.

Portfolio Requirements by Pathway

Generally, portfolios are required for User Experience (UX) students. For Data students, they are occasionally required, while for Library/Archives and Health Informatics, they are typically not mandatory.

View Path-Specific Details

Portfolio Components Explained

Front Page

Keep it clean and attractive. Feature three key projects to intrigue potential employers immediately.

About Me

Showcase your personality and background. This is where employers get to know the person behind the work.

Projects

Demonstrate your work in depth. Include your role, client, duration, and the specific challenge you solved.

Navigation

Employers have limited time. Make the user experience seamless with accessible links and clear labels.
